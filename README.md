## `01` — About Me

I'm Sathija Balachandra, an aspiring AI engineer from Sri Lanka focused on building intelligent software, exploring AI research, and engineering interactive systems.

I enjoy bridging the gap between AI models and real-world applications — from personal AI assistants and real-time computer vision to neural network visualization and data-driven intelligence platforms.

- **Current focus:** AI systems, computer vision, and software engineering.
- **Research interests:** Intelligent agents, neural networks, and human-computer interaction.
- **Engineering philosophy:** Understand the system, experiment with ideas, measure results, and iterate.
- **Long-term direction:** Develop research-driven AI systems that are practical, efficient, and genuinely useful.

> I don't just want to use intelligent systems. I want to understand how they work and build them.
